## What Is Research Visibility?

Research visibility is the degree to which your scholarly work is discoverable, accessible, and recognized across the systems that matter — from indexing databases and citation networks to academic communities and public audiences.

It is not simply about having a profile page. Visibility is a **system**, not a single action.

## The Three Dimensions of Visibility

**1. Discovery Visibility**
Can your research be found by people searching for it? This depends on how well your work is indexed across systems like Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, and OpenAlex.

**2. Identity Visibility**
Is your scholarly identity coherent and complete? Your ORCID profile, Google Scholar page, and institutional profile must all connect back to your work accurately.

**3. Communication Visibility**
Can people understand and engage with your research? Even perfectly indexed research remains invisible if it cannot be communicated beyond the specialist community.

## Why This Matters Now

The research landscape has shifted. More research is being produced than ever before. Funding decisions, collaborations, and policy impact now increasingly depend on a researcher's visibility infrastructure — not just the quality of their work.

Researchers who build strategic visibility systems consistently:
- Attract more citations
- Connect with collaborators faster
- Influence policy discussions
- Build institutional authority

## The Path Forward

The Researchvy Framework moves from Research → Visibility → Discoverability → Connection → Communication → Application → Impact.

Every step can be developed systematically. None of it requires luck — only strategy.

Start with your foundation: ensure every piece of research you have published is correctly attributed to you across all major indexing systems.
